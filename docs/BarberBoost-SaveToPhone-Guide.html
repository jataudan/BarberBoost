Add your barber to your home screen
When your barber sends you their booking link, follow these simple steps to save it as an app icon — no App Store or Google Play needed. Once saved, you can book instantly with a single tap.

1
Open the booking link in Safari
2
Tap the Share icon at the bottom
3

Android Phone
1

Tap Add to Home Screen

iPhone & iPad (iOS)
1
Open the booking link in Safari
Your barber will send you a link — tap it to open. Make sure it opens in Safari, not another browser, for the best experience.
🔗
2
Tap the Share button
Look for the ⬆ Share icon at the bottom centre of Safari. It looks like a box with an arrow pointing upward.
↑
3
Tap "Add to Home Screen"
Scroll down the Share sheet until you see Add to Home Screen. Tap it. You can edit the name if you wish.
＋
4
Tap "Add" to confirm
Tap Add in the top-right corner. The booking app icon will appear on your home screen instantly.
✓
💡 Quick tip

Once saved, the app opens full-screen without the Safari browser bar — it feels just like a real app. You can also use Chrome on iPhone and tap the ⋮ menu → Add to Home Screen.

Save to Phone — Android & Barber Guide
For Clients
Android Instructions
On Android, Chrome will often show a "Save to Phone" banner automatically at the bottom of the page. You can also add it manually:
Android (Chrome & other browsers)
1
Open the booking link
Tap the link your barber sent you. It will open in your default browser (usually Chrome). You may see a Save to Phone banner — if so, just tap it!
🔗
2
Tap the ⋮ menu
In Chrome, tap the ⋮ three-dot menu in the top-right corner of your browser. In Samsung Internet, look for the ☰ menu.
⋮
3
Tap "Add to Home Screen"
Select Add to Home Screen or Install App from the menu. Either option works — you may see one or both.
＋
4
Tap "Add" to confirm
A prompt will appear — tap Add to place the icon on your home screen. Done! Your barber's booking app is now one tap away.
